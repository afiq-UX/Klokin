Klokin Mobile Suite™

From Desk to Field —
Always in Sync.

Klokin Mobile Suite™ enables team members to perform secure, location-based check-ins directly from their mobile devices, supporting flexible and remote work environments. Each check-in is automatically synchronised with the web platform, allowing supervisors and administrators to monitor attendance in real time. This seamless integration ensures timely and accurate attendance tracking, improves oversight, and streamlines reporting for operational efficiency.

01 · Supports Pre-set & Post Approval

02 · On-the-go Schedule

Monitor and manage tasks and assignments from anywhere.

03 · Comprehensive Dashboard

View records, summaries, and key insights.

04 · Location-based Sign-in
